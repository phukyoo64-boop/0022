Ursula: An elderly woman whose primary conflict is her desperate need for recognition and control and her desire for youth and beauty, motivating her to demand executive producer credit while holding Miles and Owen hostage in a modern kitchen, and to inject a mysterious serum that transforms her into a young blonde woman, only to become entangled in Nazi bunker politics.
Hitler: A rejuvenated fascist dictator whose primary conflict is his obsessive need for control and recognition, motivating his erratic screaming at his assistant Kyle and his predatory interest in the transformed Ursula within an underground bunker.
Kyle: Hitler's beleaguered blond assistant whose primary conflict is his impossible position serving a screaming, rejuvenated dictator, motivating his silent suffering and confused reactions to the chaos around him.
Charlie: A manic dancer whose primary conflict is his desperate need to entertain and distract, motivating his frantic dancing in bunkers as bombs explode overhead and red lights flash around him.
Susan: A tech executive whose primary conflict is being targeted by violent historical figures, motivating her struggle against Adolf Hitler as he wrestles her to the ground and forces a gas mask onto her face in a dimly lit room.
Miles: A comedian tied to a kitchen counter whose primary conflict is his helplessness, motivating his desperate expressions while bound alongside Owen as Ursula demands credit and a hobo yells in the desert.
Owen: Miles's collaborator tied to a kitchen counter whose primary conflict is the same helpless captivity, motivating his shared desperation with Miles as they face Ursula's demands and the bizarre kitchen setup.
Hobo: A desert-dwelling man outside a gas station whose primary conflict is his isolation and desire to be heard, motivating his enthusiastic "Yee-haw!" outburst as he observes the bizarre events unfolding around him.
